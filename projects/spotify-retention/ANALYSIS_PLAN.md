# Product analytics case-study structure

## Decisions this project should support
- Which user segments exhibit the strongest retention or conversion opportunities?
- Where do free-tier users disengage along the product journey?
- Which lifecycle and messaging interventions are worth testing?

## Metrics and definitions to document
| Metric | Definition to verify against the workbook |
| --- | --- |
| Cohort retention | Share of a starting user cohort active again at a specified later period |
| Conversion rate | Free users becoming premium subscribers divided by an explicitly defined eligible free-user population |
| Churn rate | Users lost during a period divided by a clearly defined starting population |
| Engagement | Session frequency, listening activity, or other observed product interactions |

Different uploaded sources include user-preference, churn, revenue, and subscription data. These may have different populations and time grains. **Do not combine them into one denominator without confirming definitions and comparability.**

## Dashboard storytelling
1. Product overview and relevant KPIs
2. Retention and conversion trends
3. Segment comparisons
4. Actionable opportunities and suggested A/B tests

## Responsible claims
Treat recommendations as hypotheses until evaluated experimentally. Distinguish survey responses from observed user behavior, and label historical data periods.
