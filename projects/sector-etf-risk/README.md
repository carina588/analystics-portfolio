# Sector ETF Performance & Risk Analytics

**Professional experience overview — Terra internship**

Analyzed market data from 2018–2024 for 11 U.S. sector ETFs and the S&P 500 using Python.

### Methods
Return analysis, momentum, rolling correlation, moving averages, volatility, Sharpe/Sortino/Calmar ratios, drawdowns, beta, VaR/CVaR, and rolling VaR backtesting.

### Resume-reported results
- Average cross-sector correlation increased from **0.53** before COVID to **0.75** during 2020–2021.
- Technology (XLK) had approximately **286% cumulative return** over the analyzed period.
- Evaluated a rolling 95% VaR model over approximately **1,200 observations**.
- Developed an interactive Streamlit dashboard.
